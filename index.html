About Me

Hello! I am Qian Chen, a junior undergraduate in mechanical engineering at Zhejiang University and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign through the ZJU-UIUC Institute.

My interests focus on robotics, embodied intelligence, robot learning, motion control, automation control, and intelligent manufacturing systems. I hope to connect mechanical design, simulation modeling, learning-based control, and real-world validation in future research.

Research

Reinforcement Learning Based Locomotion Control for an Omnidirectional Wheeled-Bipedal Robot

Qian Chen, Advisor: Prof. Hua Chen, ZJUI

Robot learning, locomotion control, IsaacLab, MuJoCo, PPO, sim-to-real

Mechanical Design and Optimization for RoboMaster Robots

Qian Chen, Advisor: Prof. Jiahuan Cui, ZJUI

Mechanical design, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, 3D printing, CNC, laser cutting

Designed and optimized robot structures for chassis stability, feeding reliability, and supercapacitor protection; completed CAD iteration, fabrication, assembly validation, and competition preparation.

Automatic Timed and Quantitative Feeding Device for Single Fruit Flies

Qian Chen, Advisor: Prof. Timothy Haw-Yu Lee, ZJUI

Automation device, CAD modeling, laboratory mechanism design

Completed CAD modeling, structural design, and fabrication for a compact laboratory feeding device, improving moving-part layout, assembly convenience, and experimental stability.

Magnetic Field Assisted 3D Printing and Flexible Actuator Optimization

Qian Chen, Advisors: Prof. Peng Zhao and Prof. Chengqian Zhang, ZJU

Soft robotics, COMSOL, Arduino, magnetic actuation, 3D printing

Designed grooved magnetic-responsive flexible actuators, simulated magnetic fields with COMSOL, programmed Arduino Mega for Helmholtz coil control, and verified opening, rolling, and climbing motions.
